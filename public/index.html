BRITISH: MOVE ARMY

GAME RULES & CONTROLS

Looking for hands-on onboarding?

1 Victory Conditions

British East India Co. (Attacker): Wins immediately upon occupying all 5 Key Cities simultaneously with armies (fresh or tired):

Bombay, Hyderabad, Madras, Seringapatam, Coimbatore

Sultanate of Mysore (Defender): Wins by surviving through the end of Turn 4 without the British holding all 5 Keys, or by decimating British forces.

2 Factions & Starting Setup
  • British: Starts with 4 Fresh Armies at Bombay, Hyderabad, Madras, and Travancore + 6 tactical cards.
  • Mysore: Starts with 9 Forts at Darwar, Chitaldoorg, Mangalore, Bangalore, Seringapatam, Erode, Coimbatore, Mahé, and Dindigul + 6 tactical cards.
3 Turn Sequence (3-Phase Impulse)

The war lasts up to 4 Turns (the 4 Anglo-Mysore Wars). Play alternates in repeating 3-phase cycles:

  1. Phase 0 — British Move: Move one Fresh Army along an edge to an adjacent empty territory (army becomes Tired), attack an enemy Fort, or rest in place (tires in place).
  2. Phase 1 — Mysore Card: Play a card for its tactical power, commit Combat Strength to an ongoing battle, trade a card to reclaim an exhausted one, or pass.
  3. Phase 2 — British Card: Play a card, commit Combat Strength to the battle, trade, or pass.
Turn Refresh: When all British armies become Tired, the turn advances (+1). All Tired British armies become Fresh again, and all exhausted cards in both hands are fully replenished!
4 Combat & Siege Resolution

Moving a British army onto an enemy Fort triggers a battle. Strength is computed after both card phases:

  • Attacker (British): Adjacent British armies + committed British card strength.
  • Defender (Mysore): Adjacent Mysore forts + committed Mysore card strength.

Net Combat Strength = Attacker − Defender:

  • Net > 0 (British Victory): Defending Fort is destroyed and replaced by the Attacking Army (becomes Tired). Mysore suffers casualties and discards 1 random card.
  • Net ≤ 0 (Mysore Victory): The Fort holds. British attacker retreats or tires in place. British suffers casualties and discards 1 random card.
5 Tactical Card Powers
Mysore Sultanate Hand
  • Iron Rockets (3): +3 Combat Strength in battle, or high-tier trade.
  • Sepoy Mutiny (2): Eliminate any British army not in a Key City.
  • French Alliance (2): Deploy a new Fort adjacent to any existing Fort.
  • Monsoon (1): Turn any Fresh British army into Tired.
  • Cavalry Raid (1): Force the British to discard 1 random active card.
  • Sea Trade (1): Move any Coastal Fort to any empty territory.
British East India Co. Hand
  • Wall Breach (3): +3 Combat Strength in battle, or high-tier trade.
  • Highlanders (2): Deploy a new Fresh Army on any empty Coastal node.
  • Royal Navy (2): Transport any Army (fresh or tired) to any Coastal node.
  • Divide and Rule (1): Push an enemy Fort (not in Key City) into adjacent empty node.
  • Force March (1): Move a Tired Army into an empty node or into battle.
  • Princely States (1): Deploy a Tired Army into any empty Key City.
6 Card-to-Card Trading

During your card phase, sacrifice an active higher-value card to reclaim an exhausted card:

  • Value 3 Card: Trade to reclaim any exhausted card (Value 1 or 2).
  • Value 2 Card: Trade to reclaim any exhausted Value 1 card.
  • Value 1 Cards: Cannot be traded.
7 Controls & Interface
  • Move Army: Click an active army; destination nodes pulse gold. Click any glowing node to move. (Click unit again or "Cancel" to deselect).
  • Commit Strength: Click the round Wax Seal (+1, +2, +3) on the top-right of a card during battle.
  • Activate Ability: Click the body of an active card, then click the glowing target on the map.
  • Trade Card: Click an active card that has no board ability (glows gold), then click an exhausted card to reclaim.
  • Rest or Pass: Use the inline "Rest Unit" or "Pass Phase" buttons in the top header.
