HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS

1 Direct Army Movement

Click an Army/Fort unit to select it. Valid destination territories will pulse on the map. Click any glowing territory to move immediately.

💡 Toggle Deselection: Click the same selected unit again to instantly cancel selection.
2 Combat Strength in Battle

Single-Click the round Wax Seal number on the top-right of any card to commit its value (+1, +2, or +3) as Combat Strength in an active battle.

3 Card Ability Activation

Click the body of any active card in your hand:

  • Non-Targeted (e.g. Cavalry Raid): Executes immediately.
  • Targeted (e.g. Sepoy Mutiny, French Alliance, Highlanders): Enters Targeting Mode. Valid map territories will pulse—click one to apply.
  • No ability available? The card will automatically stage for trade instead.
4 Card-to-Card Trading

Reclaim an exhausted (greyed-out) card by trading a higher-value active card:

  1. Click an active card with no ability move (it glows gold, staged for trade).
  2. Click an eligible exhausted card to trade and reclaim it.
5 Passing & Resting

Use the inline "Pass Phase" or "Rest Unit" buttons located in the top turn header when those options are available.
