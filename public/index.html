CONTROLS

1Your Phase & Legal Actions

Follow the turn header. You can act only for the side whose phase it is; wait for the AI or online opponent during their phase. Pulsing spaces and highlighted exhausted cards show legal choices.

2Move or Rest

In the British movement phase, click a Fresh army, then a pulsing destination. To leave it in place, select it and click Rest Unit. Both actions make it Tired. Select a fort for movement only after activating an appropriate card.

3Card Abilities

Click the card body, away from its number seal.

  • Sepoy Mutiny, French Alliance, Monsoon, Highlanders, Princely States: click a pulsing map target.
  • Divide and Rule, Force March, Royal Navy, Sea Trade: click a pulsing origin, then a pulsing destination.
  • Cavalry Raid: executes immediately.
4Trade or Commit Strength

Trade: click an available card body, then the body of a highlighted, exhausted card in the same hand. You can choose a trade even if map targets are also available. Cavalry Raid executes immediately when its ability is available.

Battle strength: during your card phase, click an available card’s number seal on the left to commit its value to an active battle.

5Pass & Cancel

Pass Phase skips a card action when available. Click the selected army or card again, use Cancel, or press Escape to clear targeting. These controls do not undo completed moves. Escape closes an open drawer first.

6Settings & Modes

Open the gear for AI play, local Pass & Play, online P2P, or AI spectator mode. Changing the mode or your side starts a new game. Settings also has AI evaluation, thinking depth, board reset, and binary state copy/restore.

RULES

1Victory

British: occupy all five square keys with armies. Mysore: survive through the end of turn 4. Each turn represents one Anglo-Mysore War.

2Armies, Forts & Spaces

Red squares are British armies; green diamonds are Mysore forts. Keys are square spaces with bold uppercase labels. Only one army or fort may occupy a space. Armies normally move along a connection; forts move only through card abilities. A faded, slashed army is Tired.

3Turn & Impulse Sequence

At the start of each turn, both hands refresh and all armies become Fresh. Each impulse runs British army move → Mysore card → British card → battle resolution. The British move one Fresh army or tire it in place. Each side may use one card or pass during its card phase. Repeat until no Fresh armies remain, then begin the next turn.

4Three Uses for a Card

Spend an available card to use its printed ability, recover an exhausted card with a lower number, or add its number to an unresolved battle. Each use exhausts the card. Iron Rockets and Wall Breach have no separate map ability. All cards remain face up; discards are random.

Coast means a space touching the ocean. Card abilities can change the normal movement and deployment rules; their text describes the effect.

5Battles

An army moving into a fort starts a battle and stays at its origin until resolution. Moving the attacker or defender away cancels that battle. Count British armies and Mysore forts adjacent to the defending space, then add committed card strength. Higher strength wins; ties favor Mysore.

If the British win, remove the fort and move the army into its space. Otherwise the army stays at its origin. The losing side randomly discards a card. Battles resolve automatically after the British card phase.
